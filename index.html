    03Approach

    How I build.

    Simple UX, low friction, and decisions backed by data and customer feedback. That's what I learned in operations, and it's how I build products now.

    01

    Find the friction

    Every product starts with one question: where do people get stuck? Then I remove that step. Simple beats clever.

    02

    Fix the root cause

    Amazon taught me to fix the pattern, not just the ticket. A recurring issue is usually a product signal.

    03

    Data and people

    Numbers tell me what is happening. Customer feedback tells me why. I want both before I decide.

    04

    Ship with guardrails

    Get a working version out, QA every flow, and write down what the product will never do, like OiDoit's no-shame rule.
